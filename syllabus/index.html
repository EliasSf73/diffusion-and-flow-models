    Syllabus¶

    Forty-five topics in eight parts. The spine of the whole series is the chain

    \[ \text{DDPM} \;\to\; \text{score matching} \;\to\; \text{SDE} \;\to\; \text{probability-flow ODE} \;\to\; \text{flow matching}. \]

    Every note follows the same pattern:

    \[ \boxed{\text{Intuition} \to \text{Math} \to \text{Derivation} \to \text{Code} \to \text{Connection forward}} \]
